How to play

  1. Aim with ← → or drag across the course. Hold Shift for fine aim.
  2. Pick a club with ↑ ↓. The ring shows where a full-power shot lands with no wind.
  3. Swing with three presses of Space (or the Swing button): start the backswing, set the power, then stop the marker on the white line for accuracy.
  4. Early on the third press hooks the ball left. Late slices it right. On the green, misses push or pull the putt.
