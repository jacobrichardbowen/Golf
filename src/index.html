Workshop

Every model and texture in the game. Download one, change it in Blender (or make your own), then load it back with Replace.

How it works
  1. Models are glTF binary (.glb) in meters, +Y up, with the front facing +Z. In Blender: File › Import › glTF 2.0, then File › Export › glTF 2.0 (.glb).
  2. Your replacements are saved in this browser. Use Export my assets to back them up or move them to another browser.
  3. To build them into the game for good, put the files in the project's assets/ folder named by asset (for example tree-pine.glb) and rebuild.

How to play

  1. Walk with W A S D and look around with the mouse (click the course to capture it). Hold Shift to run. On touch screens, use the stick on the left and drag on the right.
  2. Change clubs at your bag: your caddy carries it and sets it down whenever he stops (it rides on the cart while you drive). Walk up to it, press E and click a club. Near the cart you can leave the bag strapped on the back instead.
  3. Play a shot: walk up to your ball and press E. Aim with A D or the mouse, then press Space three times: start, set power, stop on the white line.
  4. Early on the third press hooks the ball left; late slices it right. The driver's meter is fastest and curves most; the wedge and putter are slow and forgiving.
  5. Drive the cart: press E next to it to hop in, W S to drive, A D to steer, Space to brake, E to get out.
  6. Hole out, then follow the marker to the next tee.
