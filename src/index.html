      Workshop

      Every model and texture in the game. Download one, change it in Blender (or make your own), then load it back with Replace.

      How it works
      1. Models are glTF binary (.glb) in meters, +Y up, with the front facing +Z. In Blender: File › Import › glTF 2.0, then File › Export › glTF 2.0 (.glb).
      2. Your replacements are saved in this browser straight away. Commit to project puts them in the game's GitHub repository, and GitHub rebuilds Golf.html with them for everyone.
      3. Export my assets saves them as one file, to back them up or move them to another browser.

        How to play

        1. Walk with W A S D and look around with the mouse (click the course to capture it). Hold Shift to run. On touch screens, use the stick on the left and drag on the right.
        2. Change clubs at your bag: your caddy carries it and sets it down whenever he stops (it rides on the cart while you drive). Walk up to it, press E and click a club. Near the cart you can leave the bag strapped on the back instead.
        3. Play a shot: walk up to your ball and press E. Aim with A D or the mouse. Hold Space and your golfer winds up; let go and he swings. How far back he gets is how hard he hits. Hang at the top too long and he tenses up.
        4. Shape it: press Q for a close look at the club behind the ball. A D turn the face: open starts the ball right and curves it right, closed the other way. W S push the club out or pull it in: off the heel it fades, off the toe it draws, and either costs a little distance.
        5. Read the wind from the world: streaks in the air, the flags on every tee, around the course and on your caddy's back.
        6. Drive the cart: press E next to it to hop in, W S to drive, A D to steer, Space to brake, E to get out.
        7. Hole out, then follow the marker to the next tee.
        8. Play with friends: on the title screen, one player picks Host a game and reads out the four-letter code; up to two friends type it in on their own devices and join. Everyone plays their own ball at the same time, and the next hole starts when you've all holed out.
